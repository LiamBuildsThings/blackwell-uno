 Pre-repository history

Blackwell Uno was developed in Onshape before this repository existed. The versions below were summarized from Onshape version history when the repository was created (2026-11-15). The Onshape document remains the source of truth for the CAD.


Onshape version: V3, I1 (2026-11-15)
[View in Onshape] https://cad.onshape.com/documents/b8d83bfefaf1a55ff12ef1ac/w/a396a350cfd972580ded1252/e/976b522186fcf189ee8ce1e6

Overhauled the filament tensioning system, also changed the gearing system from a ~3:1 reduction to a direct drive, which i know will turn out as a failure at some point in the future, but simply, i don't have enough funds to develop this with extra features in mind, even the most basic of them like a stronger filament feeding system. 

However, i am using a Nema 17 pancake motor as the primary drive motor for the time being, simply because its a relatively lightweight and powerful stepper motor and from my experiences ive had a nice time with it. i know the circular stepper motors are more common but this printer will be anything but common.

Feel free to look at any version in onshape!
